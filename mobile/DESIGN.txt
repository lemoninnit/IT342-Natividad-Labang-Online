## Brand \& Style

The design system is centered on a **Modern Minimalist** aesthetic with a shift toward a **Monochromatic Noir** profile. It aims to evoke a sense of absolute authority, security, and quiet luxury through a stark, high-contrast dark-mode interface.

The visual narrative prioritizes brutal clarity and extreme reduction. By moving away from vibrant accents to a palette of deep blacks and mid-range grays, the system feels architectural and timeless. Interaction models are disciplined, focusing on precision typography and structural integrity rather than decorative flourishes or color-coded cues.

## Colors

The palette is strictly monochromatic, engineered for a sophisticated "Obsidian" look that maximizes contrast while remaining easy on the eyes in low-light environments.

* **Primary Gray (#777777):** Used for primary actions, success states, and active navigation indicators. It serves as the singular tonal highlight.
* **Surface Black (#000000):** Defined as the base canvas and core container color to create a true-dark background, establishing hierarchy through varying levels of gray borders.
* **Secondary Gray (#777777):** Mirrors the primary tone to ensure a unified, non-hierarchical color language.
* **Neutral Grays:** We use a tiered gray system to ensure secondary information and labels are clearly distinguished from headers.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Manrope** for its modern, geometric builds that remain highly legible at small sizes.

Headlines use a tighter letter-spacing and heavier weights to command attention, while body text maintains a generous line height for readability. Labels are set in uppercase with slight tracking to differentiate them from interactive body elements. For mobile screens, the display size is capped at 28px to ensure title strings do not wrap aggressively.

## Layout \& Spacing

The layout follows a **fluid grid** model optimized for mobile devices.

* **Margins:** A standard 20px horizontal margin is applied to the main container to ensure content does not touch the bezel.
* **Rhythm:** A 4px baseline grid governs all spatial relationships. Vertical spacing between related items (e.g., a label and an input) is 8px (xs), while spacing between unrelated card components is 16px (md) or 24px (lg).
* **Safe Areas:** All bottom navigation and fixed buttons must respect the device's native home indicator safe areas.

## Elevation \& Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Elevation** and structural outlines.

* **Level 0 (Background):** #000000. The pure black base canvas.
* **Level 1 (Cards/Surfaces):** Achieved via subtle contrast shifts.
* **Borders:** To enhance definition on dark OLED screens, cards use a 1px solid border of #777777 (often at reduced opacity) instead of shadows.
* **Interaction:** Upon press, cards should scale slightly (98%) rather than increasing shadow depth, maintaining the minimalist flat aesthetic.

## Shapes

The shape language is "Rounded," striking a balance between professional geometry and friendly accessibility.

* **Main Containers:** Cards and large buttons use a 12px-16px radius (Rounded-LG to XL).
* **Small Elements:** Tooltips and small input fields use an 8px radius.
* **Avatars/Badges:** Avatars are strictly circular (full-round). Pill badges use a maximum radius to create the "pill" effect regardless of height.

## Components

* **Bottom Navigation:** A blurred translucent background (#000000 at 80% opacity) with active icons in Primary Gray (#777777). No labels are used if icons are universally recognizable, otherwise, use Label-MD.
* **Profile Header:** Features a 64px circular avatar with a 2px gray border. Headlines are left-aligned with secondary text below.
* **Pill Badges:** Used for status (e.g., "Paid," "Pending"). High-contrast black text on a solid gray background.
* **Information Lists:** Items are separated by a subtle 1px divider (#777777 at 20% opacity). Each row has a minimum height of 56px to ensure a comfortable touch target.
* **Buttons:**

  * *Primary:* Solid Gray (#777777) background with black or white text.
  * *Secondary/Ghost:* Border-only with gray text.
  * *Destructive:* Minimalist ghost button with heavy gray text or a subtly different tonal shift.
* **Input Fields:** Filled style using the Surface color with a 1px border that clarifies to Primary Gray (#777777) on focus.
